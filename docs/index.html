Display

Depth against position along the line for the month or season shown, offshore to the left, coast to the right, at the map's horizontal scale (stations are the same distance apart on both, so the section widens as you zoom in); stations joined linearly, no smoothing. Geostrophic velocity across the line is relative to 500 dbar, from CalCOFI's dynamic heights at neighbouring stations, poleward positive. In the Anomalies view, each station and depth's departure from its climatology for the calendar month or season (20+ years).

Station values are mapped as a Gaussian-weighted mean over 75 km (e-folding scale), so contours carry no detail finer than the stations support. The field is shown only within the sampled area.

The overlay shows the dataset chosen below: fish eggs and larvae or a zooplankton dataset.
